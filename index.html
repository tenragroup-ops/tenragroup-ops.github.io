Founder Story

Tenra Group was founded by an entrepreneur from Kazakhstan who originally came to Vietnam as a traveler.

During his stay, he discovered a strong and fast-growing opportunity in the Vietnamese coffee export industry — one of the world’s key origins for green coffee beans.

Instead of returning home, he decided to stay and build a sourcing and export business directly at origin.

What started as exploration turned into a long-term commitment: creating a reliable bridge between Vietnamese producers and global coffee buyers.

Today, Tenra Group focuses on stable supply, transparent trade processes, and long-term partnerships with roasters and importers worldwide.
